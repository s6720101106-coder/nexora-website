# Phase 03 — Customer Journey

| Audience | Primary question | Content response | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individuals | Can you build my idea? | Plain-language service scope and process | Consultation / quote |
| SMEs | Will this improve my operations? | Business outcomes, examples, scope explanation | Quote |
| Large organizations | Can you support complex requirements? | Enterprise/custom software capabilities; no unverified certifications | Consultation |
| Government agencies | Can you understand formal requirements? | Clear capability descriptions and enquiry route; no unverified eligibility claims | Consultation |

## Stages
Awareness (Home/About) → Exploration (Services) → Evaluation (service detail/demo portfolio) → Intent (CTA) → Submission (form) → Follow-up (manual company response).

## Experience principles
Clear navigation, bilingual consistency, accessible forms, honest demo labels, no fabricated testimonials/case studies/certifications, and clear expectations after submission.
